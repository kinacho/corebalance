## 1. The Spanish Investor's Secret Weapon: Tax Deferral via Fund Transfer

In most countries, rebalancing an investment portfolio requires selling assets that have risen too much and buying those that have fallen. This sale triggers a capital gain on which you must immediately pay tax.

In Spain, individual investors enjoy an exceptional competitive advantage: **the fund transfer (traspaso) regime**.

Under Spanish tax law:
* You can move capital (both the principal invested and accumulated gains) from one investment fund to another **without paying tax on the gains**.
* Taxation is deferred until you eventually decide to sell your funds to withdraw cash.
* This rule applies to both traditional index funds and actively managed funds, as long as the transfer is made directly between the entities without the money passing through your current account.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **ETFs (exchange-traded funds)** do not benefit from this exemption in Spain. If you rebalance by selling one ETF to buy another, you will have to pay between 19% and 28% on the capital gains generated. This is why, for portfolios based on traditional investment funds on platforms like MyInvestor, the fund transfer is the king of rebalancing tools.

## 2. Two Paths to Rebalance on MyInvestor

When your MyInvestor portfolio drifts from its target (for example, your global equities fund now accounts for 85% instead of your target 80%), you have two options:

### Path A: Fund Transfer (When you have no new capital to invest)
Move money directly from the overweighted fund to the underweighted fund.
* **Tax treatment:** 100% tax-exempt.
* **Time:** Takes between **3 and 8 business days** to complete. During this process, your money is sold from the source fund and subscribed into the destination fund.
* **Ideal for:** Large portfolios where drifts are significant and monthly contributions are not enough to correct the deviation.

### Path B: Contribution-Based Rebalancing (When adding monthly savings)
Calculate the exact amount of your monthly savings to inject into each fund so that, when added to the existing balance, the portfolio rebalances naturally.
* **Tax treatment:** 100% tax-exempt (you sell nothing).
* **Time:** Almost instant (the usual fund subscription time, typically 1–2 business days).
* **Ideal for:** Regular contributions (monthly or quarterly) in portfolios in the accumulation phase.

## 4. How to Execute the Transfer in the MyInvestor App

Once CoreBalance has given you the exact transfer figures, execute it in MyInvestor by following these steps:

1. **Log in** to the MyInvestor website or mobile app.
2. Go to the **Investments > Investment Funds** section.
3. Find the source fund (the overweighted one you want to take money from) in your positions and click **Trade**.
4. Select the **Transfer** option (careful — don't choose *Sell*, as that will trigger the tax withholding).
5. Select the **Destination Fund** (the underweighted fund you want to inject the money into).
6. Enter the amount to transfer. You can select a **partial transfer** and enter the exact amount calculated by CoreBalance.
7. Confirm the transaction with your security code.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Rebalancing on MyInvestor

### How long will I be out of the market during the transfer?
During a domestic transfer between funds on the same platform (MyInvestor), the sale and purchase are usually chained almost consecutively. However, there may be a gap of **1 or 2 business days** where that capital is not invested in either fund. In stable markets the impact is negligible, but it's a factor to bear in mind during periods of extreme volatility.

### Is there a cost to making transfers on MyInvestor?
No. MyInvestor does not charge any purchase, sale, or transfer commissions on its open-catalogue index funds. There are also no custody fees.

### Can I transfer funds between different managers?
Yes. You can move money from a **Vanguard** index fund to an **iShares** one, or from **Fidelity** to **Amundi**, with no tax or commission restrictions.
